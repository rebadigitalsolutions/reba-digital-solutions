# Reba Digital Solution — logo direction 01

A minimal, open R shaped like a moving path: the idea is turning ideas into working digital solutions and helping businesses progress. The lowercase wordmark balances the geometric symbol with an approachable voice.

## Assets

- `reba-logo.png`: horizontal logo with transparent background, for light website backgrounds, documents, and social graphics.
- `reba-social-avatar.png`: square symbol on a light background, for social profile pictures.

Use the full logo when the descriptor remains readable; use the symbol for small placements. Preserve proportions and generous clear space. These are generated raster PNGs, not editable vector masters. The intended palette follows the website: cobalt #3155D9, ink #242825, warm white #FAF9F6; generated pixels may vary slightly.

Created with the built-in image generation tool. No website code was changed.

## Generation prompts

### Main logo

Use case: logo-brand.
Create a finished minimal professional logo for "reba" with the small descriptor "DIGITAL SOLUTION". It is a digital studio providing websites, marketing, and business automation.
Deliver ONE isolated horizontal logo lockup on a genuinely transparent background, generous but not excessive outer padding, high-resolution wide canvas. Not a presentation board.
Design concept: a distinctive geometric uppercase R symbol, built from a confident continuous ribbon/path, with a crisp negative-space opening and its diagonal leg suggesting forward movement. Meaning: bringing an idea into a working digital solution and enabling progress. The R must be immediately legible, elegantly simple, balanced, with substantial shapes that survive favicon sizes. Avoid extra arrows, extra dots, circuit traces, cursors, globes, rockets, gradients or generic tech ornament. Make a considered graphic design logo, flat vector-like edges.
Symbol on left in cobalt blue #3155D9. To its right a beautifully kerned lowercase "reba" wordmark in near-black #242825, modern humanist geometric sans-serif with subtle distinctive character, medium-bold weight. Below the wordmark, exact small descriptor "DIGITAL SOLUTION" in the same near-black, letterspaced and fully readable. Text spelling exact: r e b a, DIGITAL SOLUTION. Align descriptor width tastefully to wordmark. Icon and typography feel like one identity. No tagline, mockups, shadows, texture, borders, watermarks or additional labels. Clean transparent alpha background.

### Social avatar

Use case: logo-brand. Edit the provided logo into a square social-media avatar asset. Preserve the EXACT cobalt blue R symbol from the reference, including both diagonal strokes, its negative space, shape proportions and color. Remove all text. Center just that symbol on a flat warm off-white #FAF9F6 square background. The symbol should occupy approximately 58 percent of the canvas width, with balanced optical centering and ample circle-crop-safe margins. Crisp flat edges, solid color, no shading, gradients, texture, shadow, borders, extra shapes, labels or mockup. This must be the same brand symbol, not a redesign. High resolution square output.
